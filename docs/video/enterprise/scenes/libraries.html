What I changed by hand

(the parts a reviewer should read)

  • @acme/ui-core and @acme/ui-forms go from version 21.4.0 to 22.0.0.
  • Their Angular peer ranges go from ^21.2.0 to ^22.0.0, and @acme/ui-forms now needs @acme/ui-core ^22.0.0.

What ng update changed

Angular 22 makes components without a change-detection setting update less often by default (OnPush). The migration added ChangeDetectionStrategy.Eager to every component and test host so they keep the old behaviour.
